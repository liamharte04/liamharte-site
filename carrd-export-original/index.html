Turn founders into fundable teams

If you run an accelerator, incubator, university programme, or innovation hub, I support cohorts with practical, founder-first delivery that slots into your existing curriculum.

01 How I make impact

I embed as a mentor or specialist advisor across the cohort, deliver targeted workshops aligned to your programme milestones, and run office hours that unblock founders fast. Sessions are shaped around real outputs (pitches, decks, applications, funding plans), so founders leave with materials they can actually submit and use.

02 Common use cases

Programmes bring me in ahead of demo day, during grant and application windows, or when technical founders need help turning a product into a clear, fundable story. This is especially useful for XR, digital health, and impact tracks where evidence, ethics, and real-world delivery matter as much as vision.

03 Delivery formats

This can be delivered as a one-off workshop, a short sprint around a milestone, or ongoing cohort support across your programme. Remote or in-person both work depending on your schedule and cohort size.

  • FAQs

Common questions, honest answers

If something is unclear, book a free call or drop a message. No pressure!

Who is this for?

+

I work primarily with accelerators, incubators, university programmes, and innovation hubs supporting early-stage founders. This is especially well suited to student and early-career cohorts, where engagement, clarity, and momentum can make or break outcomes. While I occasionally support individual founders, my work is designed around the needs of programmes and cohorts first.

What makes your approach different from other mentors or speakers?

+

I’m a near-peer mentor who has recently been through the same accelerators, grants, and judging panels your founders are facing now. That proximity in age and experience means founders engage more openly, ask the real questions, and act on feedback faster. Combined with proven wins across national programmes and non-dilutive funding, this leads to sessions that land and translate into stronger submissions, not just good conversations.

How do you usually work with programmes?

+

I work as an embedded mentor or specialist advisor aligned to your programme structure. Delivery is typically built around practical workshops tied to real deadlines such as demo days, grant calls, or application rounds. Where appropriate, this is supported by structured clinics or async feedback so founders leave with clearer narratives, stronger materials, and concrete next steps they can execute immediately.

What does a typical engagement look like?

+

Engagements vary depending on the programme. Some teams bring me in for a single high-impact session around funding readiness or pitching. Others prefer a short sprint around a key milestone, or light-touch support across the programme. Formats are agreed upfront to fit your timeline, cohort size, and budget, without adding complexity or overhead.

Can you guarantee funding or competition wins?

+

Anyone who guarantees wins simply isn’t being honest. What I can do is significantly improve the quality, clarity, and alignment of pitches and applications by helping founders understand how reviewers and judges actually assess work. That’s how you meaningfully improve the odds. I’ve done this myself across multiple national programmes, and I bring that perspective directly into cohort delivery.
