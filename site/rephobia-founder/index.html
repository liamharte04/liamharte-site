Official founder profile

Liam Harte, founder of Rephobia

Building a clinician-controlled virtual reality platform with evidence, responsible facilitation and real-world delivery treated as core product work.

Origin and role

From lived experience to a governed product

Liam founded Rephobia after living with OCD and a severe fear of public speaking. The venture is developing a clinician-controlled virtual reality platform to support exposure therapy, with the therapist or responsible professional remaining in control of the experience.

As founder, Liam's work spans product direction, immersive content, clinical and academic relationships, research planning, grants, partnerships and venture strategy. Rephobia was founded in 2024 and has developed through Queen's University Belfast's entrepreneurship ecosystem.

The product remains in development. Public descriptions on this page do not claim clinical effectiveness or regulatory approval.
