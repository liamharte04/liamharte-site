About

Technology that stays accountable to people

Liam Harte is the founder of Rephobia and an immersive-technology builder. Rephobia is a clinician-controlled virtual reality platform being developed to support exposure therapy. Liam also works with accelerators, universities and innovation programmes to help early-stage founders improve pitches, funding applications and pilot plans.

His work spans digital health, XR and responsible human-centred innovation. The connecting idea is simple: ambitious technology should remain understandable, governable and useful to the people responsible for it.
